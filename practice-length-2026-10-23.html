Match practice length to the question you want answered

A long paper tests endurance. One untimed question tests whether the format makes sense. These are different learning goals.

The Gaokao mathematics practice page offers three clearly separated modes for international readers.

  • One questionUse this mode to inspect notation, translation, and the answer interface without a timer.
  • Three questionsUse the ten-minute challenge to test quick decisions under light time pressure.
  • Twenty-one questionsUse the full 2026 Shanghai Mathematics paper experience when pacing and sustained concentration matter.

Choose the smallest mode that answers your current question. A short mode is not a failed simulation. It is a focused check. Use the full paper only when timing and endurance are part of the lesson.

The public page lists 150 points and 120 minutes for the full mathematics experience. GaokaoExam.org also states that it is an independent project, not an official government publication or official English edition.
